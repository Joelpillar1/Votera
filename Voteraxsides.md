THE 4 SIDES OF VOTERAX (CLEAN & CORRECT)
1️⃣ Public / Marketing Side (No Auth)

Who it’s for: Anyone
Purpose: Trust, clarity, conversion

Includes

Landing page

How Voterax works

Use cases

Docs / transparency

Read-only campaign previews (optional)

🧠 This side sells legitimacy, not features.

2️⃣ Contributor Side (User Interface)

Who it’s for: Contributors / Participants
Purpose: Contribution → Reputation → Voting

Core Areas

Contributor dashboard

Campaign participation pages

Task submission

CP & RP visibility

Voting interface

Results & history

🧠 This is the emotional heart of the product. It must feel fair.

3️⃣ Organizer Side (Founder / Campaign Admin)

Who it’s for: Campaign creators
Purpose: Design & manage fair processes

Core Areas

Organizer dashboard

Campaign creation flow

Task & CP definition

Contribution review

Voting management

Result finalization

🧠 This side must feel powerful but restrained.

Important:
Organizer ≠ System Admin

4️⃣ System Admin Side (Internal Only)

Who it’s for: Voterax internal team
Purpose: Integrity & safety

Core Areas

Campaign registry

CP/RP ledger

Abuse detection

Audit logs

On-chain anchoring

Role & permission overrides

🧠 No beauty required. Pure function.

🚫 WHAT YOU DO NOT NEED (Very Important)

You do NOT need:

A separate “Moderator” role (Organizer handles moderation)

A “Super Admin” beyond system admin

Separate mobile vs desktop products (responsive is enough)

Multiple contributor tiers (RP already handles hierarchy)

Adding more sides would:

Increase cognitive load

Dilute fairness

Break the elegance of the system
